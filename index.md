I'm **Sara**, a PhD student at the Animal Flight Lab, Lund University. My work here focuses on combining GPS data and experimental wind tunnel data to gain a better understanding of **Vertebrate migration**

Prior to this, I completed my Masters in Ecology and Conservation at Uppsala University.Here I completed my thesis project, *Seabird mediated spatiotemporal distriution of pelagic fish in the Baltic Sea*, in collaboration with the Baltic Seabird Project, where I focused on modelling the horizontal and vertical distribution of pelagic fish in proximity to a guillemot colony using echosounder derived data. 

I completed my **BSc (Hons) in Biology** at the University of St. Andrews, where my thesis looked at the *Effect of anthropogenic disturbances on the escape behaviour in Eurasian Oystercatchers*
